01 / THE LEAGUE

The 2026–2027
World League

Sixteen country teams compete in the inaugural season, with two men and two women on each roster. Atlantic and Pacific each have eight teams. Each country plays seven conference opponents, then a CrossFire matchup against the other conference at the end of Event 4.

Teams play six regulation games in each matchup: men’s doubles, women’s doubles, and four mixed pairings. At 3–3, teams play EPL PointBreak to decide the winner. Regular-season results set each team’s conference seed for the February 27 World Championship. All 16 teams advance to that single-elimination event.

Match format

Six regulation games per matchup,
with PointBreak at 3–3.

2+2

Two men and two women

Each country team has two men and two women. M1 and M2 identify the two men; W1 and W2 the two women. Teams play six regulation games, combining men’s, women’s, and all four mixed pairings. Each player plays three regulation games per matchup and six per regular-season event.

THE MATCH DECIDER · AT 3–3

EPL PointBreak

Both mixed pairs play one game
with a shared score.

Teams play PointBreak if they tie 3–3 after six regulation games. Both mixed pairs take part, so each team uses all four players.

  1. 01

    First pairs start

    The first mixed pair from each team starts at 0–0 and plays until one team reaches 6 points.

  2. 02

    Switch at 6

    Both teams’ second mixed pairs take over. The score stays exactly where it is.

  3. 03

    Finish to 11

    The second pairs continue the same game to 11, win by 2. The winning country wins the matchup.

SCORE EXAMPLE

If the first pairs reach 6–4, the second pairs begin at 6–4. At 10–10, play continues until one team leads by two.

Organizers record and submit the six regulation games to DUPR. PointBreak determines the matchup winner. Organizers do not record its score or include it in DUPR submissions or point differential.

OUR STORY / ELITE PICKLEBALL

About Elite Pickleball

Tournaments and leagues
based in the Midwest.

VETERAN-OWNED PICKLEBALL ORGANIZATION

Pickleball tournaments
and league play

Elite Pickleball (EP) is a veteran-owned organization that runs pickleball tournaments and the Elite Pickleball World League.

Billy Kinney founded Elite Pickleball, and Jen Limjoco serves as Executive Director. EP has organized events in the Midwest for about four years.

We’ve hosted more than 200 tournaments, with players traveling from nine states. We offer divisions for recreational players and advanced competitors.

01 / THE COMPETITION

Events for a range
of skill levels

We run men’s, women’s, and mixed doubles divisions for players from 3.0 through 5.0+, including advanced and professional-level competitors.

Our cash-prize events include the Midwest Showdown, which has featured Kyle Yates, Ammar Wazir, Robert Slutsky, and Miles Hoover. Established professionals compete alongside emerging players.

In 2026, we expect to distribute more than $50,000 in cash prizes, merchandise, giveaways, and community awards across our events.

02 / THE WORLD LEAGUE

16 country teams
competing for a championship

In the Elite Pickleball World League (EPL), 64 athletes represent 16 countries.

Players compete in four regular-season events before all 16 teams advance to the World Championship. We track team results and individual player statistics throughout the season.

Each country has a four-player roster with two men and two women.
