ABOUT THE LEAGUE

EPL World League

Elite Pickleball’s 2026–2027 inaugural season brings together 16 country teams, each with two men and two women. Eight teams compete in the Atlantic Conference and eight in the Pacific Conference. Across four regular-season events, each country plays eight matchups: seven within its conference and one cross-conference matchup.

Each country matchup includes six regulation games: men’s doubles, women’s doubles, and all four mixed pairings. A 3–3 tie is settled by EPL PointBreak. All 16 countries advance to the World Championship on February 27, seeded within their conferences for a single-elimination bracket.

THE MATCH DECIDER · AT 3–3

EPL PointBreak

Two mixed pairs per team.
One continuous score.

When the six regulation games finish tied at 3–3, PointBreak decides the team match. Each team uses both of its mixed pairs, so all four players take part.

  1. 01

    First pairs start

    The first mixed pair from each team starts at 0–0 and plays until one team reaches 6 points.

  2. 02

    Switch at 6

    Both teams’ second mixed pairs take over. The score stays exactly where it is.

  3. 03

    Finish to 11

    The second pairs continue the same game to 11, win by 2. The winning country wins the matchup.

SCORE EXAMPLE

If the first pairs reach 6–4, the second pairs begin at 6–4. At 10–10, play continues until one team leads by two.

Only the six regulation games are recorded and submitted to DUPR. PointBreak contributes a matchup winner only; its score is not recorded, submitted to DUPR, or included in point differential.
