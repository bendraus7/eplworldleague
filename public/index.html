01 / THE LEAGUE

A world league.
A local stage.

Elite Pickleball’s 2026–2027 inaugural season brings together 16 country teams, each with two men and two women. Eight teams compete in the Atlantic Conference and eight in the Pacific Conference. Across four regular-season events, each country plays eight matchups: seven within its conference, followed by CrossFire against the other conference in the final matchup of Event 4.

Each country matchup includes six regulation games: men’s doubles, women’s doubles, and all four mixed pairings. A 3–3 tie is settled by EPL PointBreak. Overall regular-season results determine each country’s conference seed for the World Championship on February 27. All 16 countries advance; the single-elimination championship decides the league winner.

Match format

with the EPL PointBreak finish.

Two men and two women.

Each country team has two men and two women. M1 and M2 identify the two men; W1 and W2 the two women. Teams play six regulation games, combining men’s, women’s, and all four mixed pairings. Each player plays three regulation games per matchup and six per regular-season event.

THE MATCH DECIDER · AT 3–3

EPL PointBreak

Two mixed pairs per team.
One continuous score.

When the six regulation games finish tied at 3–3, PointBreak decides the team match. Each team uses both of its mixed pairs, so all four players take part.

  1. 01

    First pairs start

    The first mixed pair from each team starts at 0–0 and plays until one team reaches 6 points.

  2. 02

    Switch at 6

    Both teams’ second mixed pairs take over. The score stays exactly where it is.

  3. 03

    Finish to 11

    The second pairs continue the same game to 11, win by 2. The winning country wins the matchup.

SCORE EXAMPLE

If the first pairs reach 6–4, the second pairs begin at 6–4. At 10–10, play continues until one team leads by two.

Only the six regulation games are recorded and submitted to DUPR. PointBreak contributes a matchup winner only; its score is not recorded, submitted to DUPR, or included in point differential.

OUR STORY / ELITE PICKLEBALL

About Elite Pickleball

Where competition
meets opportunity.

VETERAN-OWNED. COMMUNITY-DRIVEN.

Elevating competition.
Building community.
Creating opportunity.

Elite Pickleball (EP) is a veteran-owned sports organization dedicated to elevating competitive pickleball through professionally organized tournaments, innovative league play, and exceptional player experiences.

Founded by Billy Kinney, with Jen Limjoco serving as Executive Director, Elite Pickleball has spent approximately four years establishing itself as a respected presence in the Midwest pickleball community.

With more than 200 tournaments hosted and competitors traveling from nine different states, Elite Pickleball has built its reputation on competitive excellence, quality events, and a commitment to providing opportunities for players at every level.

Our leadership shares a vision of creating something greater than individual tournaments—a competitive organization where athletes, sponsors, and communities come together through their passion for pickleball.

01 / THE COMPETITION

A tradition of
competitive excellence.

From developing players to elite-level competitors, Elite Pickleball offers a diverse range of events designed to challenge, inspire, and bring the pickleball community together.

Our tournaments feature men’s, women’s, and mixed doubles competitions across skill divisions ranging from 3.0 recreational players to 5.0+ advanced and professional-level competitors.

Our premier cash-prize competitions, including the Midwest Showdown, have featured elite players such as Kyle Yates, Ammar Wazir, Robert Slutsky, and Miles Hoover. These events bring together established professionals and emerging talent, delivering memorable matchups and showcasing high-level competitive pickleball.

In 2026 alone, Elite Pickleball expects to distribute more than $50,000 in cash prizes, merchandise, giveaways, and community awards, reflecting our commitment to reinvesting in the players and communities that support our organization.

02 / THE WORLD LEAGUE

Sixteen nations.
One league.
One championship.

Continuing our mission to innovate and expand competitive opportunities, Elite Pickleball proudly introduces the Elite Pickleball World League (EPL).

Featuring 16 countries, 16 teams, and 64 athletes, the World League brings a new dimension to competitive pickleball through international team representation, season-long competition, individual player statistics, and a championship format.

The league celebrates national pride, teamwork, and individual achievement while providing players with a professional-style competitive experience.
