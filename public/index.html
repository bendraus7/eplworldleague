ABOUT THE LEAGUE

EPL World League

Elite Pickleball’s 2026–2027 inaugural season brings together 16 country teams, each with two men and two women. Eight teams compete in the Atlantic Division and eight in the Pacific Division.

Each team match includes men’s doubles, women’s doubles, and two mixed doubles games. A 2–2 tie is settled by an EPL Point Break.

HOW IT WORKS

Match format

MLP-style team competition,
with the EPL Point Break finish.

2+2

Each country team has two men and two women. Teams face off across four doubles games, combining men’s, women’s, and mixed competition.
