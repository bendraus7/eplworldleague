Preserve the globe's emerald-green landmasses, dark almost-black oceans, fine glowing green latitude/longitude grid, rich glossy shading and subtle illuminated green rim. Remove all EPL lettering, world league lettering, shield, metallic border, stars, ribbon, and pickleball. Reconstruct a full clean globe in the same style on a pure black background. No text, no logo lettering, no border, no other objects. Landscape 3:2 canvas. A single large globe viewed toward the Atlantic, Americas left and Europe/Africa right, centered slightly above the middle and occupying about 90 percent of image height, with black margins, green luminosity strongest on upper-left rim, moody black lower globe. This will be a decorative background behind two overlapping player cards in a sports league homepage, so prioritize a recognizable circular green globe with visible continents toward the top and sides, and leave bottom edge black for overlaid interface text. Finished clean professional brand background.
